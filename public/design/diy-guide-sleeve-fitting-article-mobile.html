PVC SLEEVE INSTALLATION

Difficulty EXPERT (LEVEL 4)
Estimated Time 45 MINUTES

The drift sleeve is the soul of electric sliding. Mastering the fitment process ensures maximum adhesion and heat dissipation during high-speed technical maneuvers. This guide covers the precision heat-shrink technique required for E-Drift performance.

build Required Toolset
expand_more

01

Surface Prep

precision_manufacturing

Diagram 4-A: Hub Clearance

Clean the hub surface using isopropyl alcohol. Any residue will create air pockets that compromise the sleeve's structural integrity under friction.

02

Heat Activation

"Maintaining a constant 230°F is critical. Oscillate the heat gun 4 inches from the PVC surface to avoid localized melting."

— Lead Engineer, Drift Lab

Uniformly heat the sleeve until it achieves a semi-malleable state. Slide onto the hub in one fluid motion to prevent uneven cooling cycles.
