TECH SPECS
& FAQ

Deep-dive technical knowledge for maintenance and performance optimization.

01. BATTERY SAFETY & STORAGE expand_more
  • Always store batteries at 40-60% charge for long-term inactivity.
  • Use only the official VOLT DRIFT high-current balancing charger.
  • Operating temperature range: -5°C to 45°C. Performance may drop below zero.
02. REAR SLEEVE MAINTENANCE expand_more

Polypropylene sleeves are consumables. Check for cracks every 5 hours of drift time. Replace immediately if core wheel exposure exceeds 15%.

03. MOTOR TUNING & FIRMWARE expand_more

Connect via USB-C to the VOLT DRIFT Configurator. Current limiters can be adjusted for "Expert Mode," but this may void warranty on standard motor coils.
